## Notes

- **The gazetteer mixes licences.** Of 784 entries, 424 are ODbL (OpenStreetMap), 59 are GODL-India (India Post) and 301 come from government documents with no stated licence (75 wards and 226 polling stations). Each entry says which. If you publish the file, the ODbL terms apply to the whole database and the others must be allowed to sit inside it.
- **Government sources with no stated licence.** We use them as public information for a prototype, say where each comes from, and do not claim them as open data. Before a public release or a pilot, ask JMC, JSCL and the Election Commission for permission in writing.
- **Credits.** [NOTICE](../NOTICE) names MapLibre, the voices, OpenStreetMap and the other sources. Keep it in step with this table.
